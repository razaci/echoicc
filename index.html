FIELD MANUAL

How to play ECHO

1. Begin your expedition

You start with a balanced deck drawn from the 90-card Ivory & Copper collection. Defeat twelve guardians to reach the Crown of the Living Ark. Each victory lets you add a new card or heal.

2. Spend energy on cards

Every turn begins with three energy and a hand of five cards. Strike cards damage the guardian. Ward cards create guard to absorb attacks. Insight cards help you draw or gain energy. Ritual cards apply special effects. Ally cards summon defenders into one of three lanes.

3. Command the three lanes

Each lane can hold one ally. An ally strikes at the end of your turn, and stays until defeated. The guardian's attack may hit your allies, or your vitality if no ally intercepts. Selecting a new ally for an occupied lane replaces the old ally.

4. Read the guardian's intention

Every guardian reveals its next action: attack, heavy strike, ward, or siphon. Plan around it. Your guard resets after each enemy action, so timing matters.

5. Learn from the cards

Each card is linked to a lesson: a physical landmark, a perception lens, and macro, micro, and nano questions. The Codex lets you read the original lesson metadata while you play.

6. Save your progress

Expeditions autosave on this device using browser storage. A new run resets the battle but keeps the card archive available. No network connection is required.

Original ECHO gameplay. Familiar deckbuilding and turn-based strategy conventions are used, but the game rules, story, and presentation are custom to this project.
